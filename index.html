What's not included in a standard cleaning?

The interior of cabinets, the fridge and oven, the garage, the patio, windows, and laundry/ironing are not part of either the deep or regular cleaning — these are add-on services we quote separately based on what you need.

Frequently asked questions

Before you message us, this might already answer your question

Do you charge by the hour or by service?

Cleaning services are charged per service, based on the condition of the space at the time of the visit. Additional services, like laundry and organization, are charged by the hour.

How is the price calculated?

The price depends on the square footage of the space, the type of service chosen, and the agreed frequency.

What areas do you work in?

We cover all of Broward County and Miami-Dade.

Do you work on weekends?

Yes. We apply an extra charge on Sundays and holidays.

Do you bring your own cleaning products?

It depends on what we agree with each client: we can bring our own products or use yours, whichever you prefer.

How long does a cleaning take?

It depends on the type of service, the size of the space, and the condition it's in.

Do I need to be home?

It's not necessary. We coordinate access in whatever way is most convenient for you.

How do I pay?

We accept check, Zelle, and credit card.
